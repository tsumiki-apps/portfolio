制作の背景BACKGROUNDBackground制作の背景

もともと、稽古の現場では「今日は誰が来られないか」が分かるたびに、香盤表とにらめっこして『じゃあ今日はどの場面をやろう』と組み直していました。この照合が毎回じわじわ時間を食い、稽古の頭出しでもたついてしまう。

その“段取りのストレス”をなくすために、香盤メーカーは生まれました。欠席者を選ぶだけで「今日できる場面」が出るなら、迷っていた時間はまるごと稽古に回せる——そう考えて形にした一本です。

そのあと実際の稽古場でお使いいただくうちに、分かったことがありました。休みは終日だけではない——「◯時入り」「◯時出し」がある。そして稽古時間を前半・後半で区切ると、できる場面が変わる。いまの香盤メーカーは、その現場の声をそのまま入れた2代目です。出られない日の表（てっぱり表）をアプリの中に持ち、時間帯ごとに判定し、稽古全体の進み具合まで見えるようにしました。In rehearsal rooms, every time it became clear who couldn't come that day, the team would pore over the scene breakdown and rebuild the plan: “So which scenes can we do today?” That cross-checking quietly ate up time again and again, and rehearsals were slow to get started.

Kouban Maker was created to take that scheduling stress away. If simply selecting the absent cast members showed which scenes were possible, all the time spent deliberating could go straight back into rehearsal — that was the idea behind it.

Once it was in use in an actual rehearsal room, a few things became clear. Absences aren't always for the whole day — people arrive late or leave early. And dividing rehearsal time into first and second halves changes which scenes are possible. Today's Kouban Maker is the second version, built directly on that feedback from the rehearsal room. It keeps the conflict chart (who can't attend when) inside the app, checks each time slot, and even shows how far the whole rehearsal process has progressed.
